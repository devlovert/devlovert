history of hazrat muhammad

Muhammad (c. 570–632 CE), the founder of Islam, was an Arab religious, social, and political leader born in Mecca. Orphaned young, he worked as a merchant, earning a reputation for integrity. In 610, he began receiving divine revelations, forming the Quran and initiating the monotheistic religion of Islam. After facing persecution,according to islam he migrated to Medina (622), established an Islamic state, and eventually unified Arabia before his death.
